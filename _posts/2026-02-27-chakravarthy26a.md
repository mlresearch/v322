abstract: 'We propose MultiPersona-Align, a novel approach for personalized image
  generation that enhances multi-subject diffusion models through self-supervised
  feature alignment. While existing methods rely primarily on spatial masking for
  subject control, they often produce semantically inconsistent features that fail
  to preserve the subject-specific visual characteristics. Our method introduces Dual
  Alignment Framework: (1) Spatially-Aligned Subject-Specific Cross-Attention Mechanism
  that aligns subject-specific diffusion features with corresponding DINOv2 CLS tokens
  within spatial regions, and (2) Patch-Aligned Self-Attention that ensures global
  semantic consistency by aligning full-image diffusion features with DINOv2 patch
  representations. This approach leverages DINOv2’s robust semantic understanding
  without requiring additional training data or annotations. Experiments on multi-subject
  generation tasks demonstrate that our alignment losses significantly improve subject
  fidelity and semantic consistency while maintaining spatial control. The method
  integrates seamlessly into existing architectures, adding minimal computational
  overhead during training while providing substantial quality improvements in personalized
  image generation.'
